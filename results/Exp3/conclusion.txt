Due to the limitation of VRAM, the FP16 model cannot be loaded. Using FP8 as the high-precision benchmark and comparing it with AWQ 4-bit. Both models belong to the Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct series, and the hardware and vLLM parameters are exactly the same.

1.Hpothesis
AWQ model condense the weights to the half of original version, so the decode time might be lower and TTFT may similar to the original version. Because quantization mainly reduces weight storage size rather than changing model architecture, TTFT was expected to remain relatively close to the FP8 model. The total accuracy may be undermined due to less weight metrices. The overall through output and GPU VRAM could be improved.

2.Result Analysis
(1)The main bottleneck in this experiment is decoding time. FP model have a better decoding time performance( 200~300% improvement). The improved decode efficiency of the FP8 model increases overall throughput and reduces system pressure, which contributes to lower TTFT under concurrent workloads. The 4-bit weights of AWQ need to be dequantized back to high precision before each matrix multiplication. This operation occurs in every step of the decode process, and the cumulative overhead results in the decode time being three times that of FP8.
(2)AWQ reduces VRAM usage by storing model weights in lower-bit quantized representations.
(3)The accuracy difference remains relatively small because AWQ preserves most model behavior despite quantization approximation.

3.Difference With Hypothesis
The result is different with hypothesis. For low-end GPU, the main bottleneck is VRAM for KV cache. In this experiment, VRAM is enough for FP8 model. Hence, the dequantied cost cannot be ignore and decoding time become the main bottleneck.

4.Applicable Conditions
The following conditions make FP8 model applicable:
(1)There are modern GPUs that support FP8 calculations.
(2)There is online reasoning that is sensitive to delays.
(3)The VRAM is abundant, so there is no need for extreme compression of the weights.
The following condition make AWQ model applicable:
Extremely limited VRAM (for consumer-grade GPUs and edge devices)

5.Conclusion
On GPUs with FP8 hardware support, AWQ 4-bit does not have a speed advantage over FP8. The decode time deteriorates by 3 times, the throughput decreases by 60-63%, and the accuracy difference is negligible (0.84%). The value of AWQ lies in extremely limited memory scenarios rather than in production environments that pursue inference speed. On GPUs with FP8 hardware support and sufficient VRAM capacity, AWQ 4-bit does not show inference speed advantages over FP8 in this experiment. The performance gap may also be influenced by hardware-specific FP8 acceleration support on modern NVIDIA GPUs.
